# In Shenzhen, I celebrated my own birthday.

When under heavy work pressure, there seems to be an urge for revenge shopping. Things that I usually can't afford to buy suddenly become desirable, and places that I usually deem unnecessary also attract my interest, as if using the money spent and the short period of happiness to get rid of accumulated fatigue.

When I first came to Shenzhen, I was mostly caught up in the flow of people and work. I followed everyone to commute, work, and return home every day, without really taking the time to explore the city or celebrate my birthday properly.

Fortunately, someone noticed my unhappiness.

## Only the first delicious dinner is enough.

I'm glad I have a girlfriend like this. She probably knows I'm under a lot of pressure at work lately, and she understands that what I need isn't a grand celebration, just a break from work to have a meal and walk around.

In the evening, we went to a restaurant that looked great and had high ratings. I was really looking forward to ordering, and the food was indeed perfect for photos: the presentation was meticulous, the colors were vibrant, and the table was quickly filled up.

Unfortunately, the taste doesn't quite match the appearance. The most accurate description would be: only the first bite is good.

At first, it seemed pretty good, but as I continued eating, the initial surprise faded away. Fortunately, what really matters for this birthday meal isn't a rating, nor is it necessary to eat at a restaurant that deserves repeated recommendations. It's already enough that someone is willing to sit here with me, listen to me talk about the recent pressures, and make this evening different from an ordinary end-of-work night.

Of course, since the photos were taken, I still want to keep this meal.

## Walking through the night in Shenzhen

After eating, we didn't rush to go back, but instead strolled around nearby, using the walk as a way to relax.

The nightscapes of Shenzhen are very different from during the day. During the day, what we see are hurried people, office buildings, and endless tasks; at night, when the lights turn on and the edges of buildings are redefined, the city suddenly has a distance suitable for viewing.

It is only at this time that I realize I am not just here to work in Shenzhen, but also truly part of its nightlife.

Walking doesn’t solve the stress of work, and the tasks to be faced the next day remain there. But the emotions seem to loosen a bit. Sometimes people don’t need to think about all problems immediately; just stepping out from the original rhythm, breathing in the wind, looking at the lights, and then walking back slowly is already a effective break.

## Even watching two movies

On the second day, I went to see two movies.

One screening is in IMAX because the movie only shows there; the other screening is at a small cinema near my home. I knew before that there were differences between cinemas, but I never expected the ticket prices would vary so much—the actual experience can be so different.

IMAX screens are very clear, and the sound is also impactful enough. When watching "A Moment in Time", the visuals and music blend together, easily immersing you in the experience. Those grand and bright scenes paired with powerful sound create a sense of awe that cannot be replaced by computers or phones.

Another small cinema offers a completely different experience. The screen is a bit blurry, and the film "Four Crossings" also has many shaky shots, making it feel jittery and even a bit dizzy after watching.

Watching two movies on the same day makes the difference very evident. The more expensive tickets offer not only a larger screen but also better clarity, sound, and a more immersive experience. Of course, small theaters have the advantage of being closer to home, making it easy to watch them on demand. There is no choice that suits every situation, but if I encounter a movie I really look forward to in the future, I might be more willing to pay for a better theater.

## It doesn’t matter if it’s a bit later.

This birthday wasn’t strictly on the birthday date, but more like a time granted to oneself after a busy day.

A dinner with only the first bite stunning, a random night walk, and two movies with completely different experiences. Each on its own would be just an ordinary thing, but together they allow me to briefly step away from work and commuting.

When I first came to Shenzhen, I always felt pushed forward by the city. Everyone moves quickly, and I don’t dare stop. But life cannot always be about adaptation, work, and rushing. Sometimes spending a little more money on a meal or a movie I truly enjoy might not solve practical problems, but it reminds me that I am here not just for work, but to live here too.

This time, someone accompanied me to slow down and celebrated my birthday together.

That’s just right.
