# In Shenzhen, I celebrated my own birthday

When under heavy work pressure, it seems we always have an urge for a little revenge shopping. Things we usually can't afford to buy suddenly become appealing, and places we usually deem unnecessary also attract our interest, as if we want to use the money spent and the fleeting joy to get rid of some accumulated fatigue.

When I first came to Shenzhen, I was mostly driven forward by the crowd and work. I followed everyone to commute, work, and return home every day, without actually taking the time to explore the city or celebrate my birthday properly.

Fortunately, someone noticed my unhappiness.

## Only the first meal is delicious

I'm glad I have a girlfriend like this. She probably knows I’m under a lot of pressure at work lately, and she also understands that what I need isn’t a grand celebration, just a short break from work to have a meal and some walking together.

In the evening, we went to a restaurant that looked great and had high ratings. I was quite excited when we ordered, and the food indeed suited great for photos: the presentation was meticulous, the colors were vibrant, and the table quickly filled up.

Unfortunately, the taste doesn’t fully match the appearance. The most accurate description would be: only the first bite is good.

At first, it seemed pretty good, but as I continued eating, the initial surprise faded away. Fortunately, what truly matters for this birthday meal isn’t a rating, nor is it necessary to eat at a restaurant that deserves repeated recommendations. It’s already enough that someone is willing to sit here with me, listen to me talk about my recent pressures, and make this evening different from an ordinary end-of-workday night.

Of course, since the photos were taken, we still want to keep this meal.

## Walking into Shenzhen at Night

After dinner, we didn’t rush to go back, but instead strolled around nearby, using the walk as a way to relax.

The nightscenes in Shenzhen are very different from during the day. During the day, what we see are hurried people, office buildings, and endless tasks; at night, when the lights turn on and the edges of buildings are redefined, the city suddenly has a distance suitable for viewing.

It is only at this time that I realize I am not just here to work in Shenzhen, but also truly part of its nightlife.

Walking doesn't solve the stress of work, and the tasks to be faced the next day are still there. But the emotions seem to loosen a bit. Sometimes people don't need to figure out all the problems immediately; just temporarily stepping out of the original rhythm, breathing in the wind, looking at the lights, and then walking back slowly, is already a effective rest.

## Watching two movies in a row

On the second day, I watched two movies.

One screening is in IMAX because the movie only shows there; the other screening is at a small cinema near my house. I knew before that there would be differences between cinemas, but I never expected the ticket prices would vary so much—the actual experience can differ significantly.

IMAX screens are very clear, and the sound is also impactful enough. When watching "A Moment in Time", the visuals and music blend together, easily immersing you in the experience. Those grand and bright scenes paired with powerful sound create a sense of awe that cannot be replaced by computers or phones.

Another small cinema offers a completely different experience. The screen is a bit blurry, and the film “Four Crossings” has many shaky shots, making it feel jittery and even a bit dizzy after watching.

Watching two movies on the same day shows a significant difference. The more expensive tickets offer not only a larger screen but also better clarity, sound, and a more immersive experience. Of course, small theaters have the advantage of being closer to home, making it convenient for last-minute viewing. There is no choice that suits every situation, but if I encounter a movie I really look forward to in the future, I might be more willing to pay for a better theater.

## It doesn’t matter if it’s a bit later

This birthday wasn’t strictly on the birthday date, but more like a time granted to oneself after a busy day.

A dinner with only the first bite stunning, a random night walk, and two movies with completely different experiences. Each on its own would be just ordinary things, but combined, they allow me to step away from work and commuting for a short while.

When I first came to Shenzhen, I always felt pushed forward by the city. Everyone moves quickly, and I don’t dare stop. But life cannot always consist only of adaptation, work, and hustle. Sometimes spending a little more money to have a meal or watch a truly enjoyable movie might not solve practical problems, but it reminds me that I am here not just for work, but to live there.

This time, someone has accompanied me to slow down and celebrated my birthday.

That’s just right.
