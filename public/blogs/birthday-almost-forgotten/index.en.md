# Almost forgot my birthday

During my university years, I was very busy and didn’t have many photos or many people I got to know deeply.

But now, looking back, every person I met was important. The group photos in the album might not be well-captured, and some even have no memory of what happened before or after, but just by taking another look, the classrooms, labs, and the lively atmosphere during gatherings seem to come back to me gradually.

![Group photo with friends](/images/pictures/810c7a01103bde6fd8eccad2b5c5c548131a3acc6d07bed1f7a989322c31d51c.webp)

## A Day Spent by Work

This year’s birthday was rather unusual.

In the morning, when I saw the message “Happy Birthday” from my father, my first thought was: Did they misremember the date?

Then I went to work as usual, focusing on my tasks. The day was filled with work, with no cake or planned outings. By the time I finished my shift at night, I was on my way home at 11:40. My mother asked me, “It’s your birthday today—where did you go?”

I was momentarily confused, and only then remembered that it really is today.

The saddest thing in the world might be like this: on your birthday, everyone at home remembers, but you forget due to your busyness.

With only twenty minutes left until midnight, I quickly pulled out a few photos from my album to post on Moments, at least to leave something for this day. The photos aren’t chosen yet, and the words aren’t decided, but the birthday has already passed.

Ah, I’ve aged another year.

## Photos from a college years ago in the album

While looking for photos, I accidentally stumbled upon some images from my college days.

Some people participated in activities together, others worked in the lab, and many gathered around a table to eat. In the past, I always thought these scenes were ordinary, that after the gathering everything would end, and after the competition, the events would also conclude. Now, looking back, I realize that those who were willing to work and take photos together were actually a precious part of that time.

The statement "University is very busy" is not entirely a complaint. There is busyness in attending classes, working on projects, and participating in competitions. Often, people only focus on solving immediate problems and rarely realize that those ordinary days will become memories later on.

Photos left behind some things that I didn’t record carefully at the time. Everyone in the picture is crowded together, smiling at the camera, possibly with unfinished tasks behind them or having just finished a busy period. What exactly happened may blur over time, but seeing those familiar faces reminds me of how I once worked hard with a group of people on certain things.

I didn’t meet many people during my university years, and I’m not particularly good at maintaining lively interpersonal relationships. But the number doesn’t seem to matter much. What truly stays in memory is not how long the contact list is, but who stood beside me during a specific period of time.

## Grew another year old

This year I didn’t truly celebrate my birthday, and almost missed it. But in the last twenty minutes looking through my photos, I unexpectedly reviewed the past again.

My parents remember my birthday, and old photos remind me of my university days. It wasn’t until the end of the day that I finally remembered to stop and take a look at myself.

A birthday doesn’t lose its meaning just because I missed midnight. It simply reminds me that no matter how busy work is, life shouldn’t consist only of pending tasks. Those who care about me, those who walked this path with me, and myself who is striving to live today, are all worth being remembered seriously.

Next time, I hope we won't have to wait until 10:40 p.m. to realize that this day belongs to us.
