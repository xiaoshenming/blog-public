# Almost forgot my birthday

During my university years, I was very busy and didn’t have many photos or many people I got to know well.

But now, looking back, every person I met was important. The group photos in the album might not be well taken, and some even don’t remind me what happened around them, but just by looking again, the classrooms, labs, and the lively atmosphere at gatherings seem to come back to me gradually.

![Group photo with friends](/images/pictures/810c7a01103bde6fd8eccad2b5c5c548131a3acc6d07bed1f7a989322c31d51c.webp)

## A Day Spent by Work

This year’s birthday was rather unusual.

In the morning, when I saw the message “Happy Birthday” from my father, my first thought was: Did they misremember the date?

Then I went to work as usual, focusing on my tasks. The day was filled with work, with no cake or planned outings. By the time I finished work at night and was on my way home, it was already 11:40. My mother asked me, “It’s your birthday today—where did you go?”

I was momentarily confused, and only then remembered that it really was today.

The saddest thing in the world is probably this: on your birthday, everyone at home remembers, but you forget due to your busyness.

With only twenty minutes left until midnight, I quickly pulled out a few photos from my album to post on Moments, at least to leave something for this day. The photos aren’t chosen yet, and the words aren’t thought out, but the birthday has already passed.

Ah, I’ve aged another year.

## Photos from the college years in the album

While searching for photos, I accidentally found some images from my college days.

Some people participated in activities together, others worked in the lab, and many gathered around a table to eat. In the past, I thought these scenes were ordinary, that they just happened after the event or competition ended. Now, looking back, I realize that those who were willing to work and take photos together were actually a precious part of that time.

Photos left behind some things that I didn’t record carefully at the time. Everyone in the picture is crowded together, smiling at the camera, possibly with unfinished tasks behind them or just after a busy period. What exactly happened may become blurred over time, but seeing those familiar faces reminds me of how I once worked hard with a group of people on certain things.

I didn’t meet many people during my university years, and I’m not particularly good at maintaining lively interpersonal relationships. But the number doesn’t seem to matter much. What truly stays in memory is not how long the contact list is, but who stood beside me during a specific period of time.

## Grew another year old

This year I didn’t take my birthday seriously, and almost missed it. But in those last twenty minutes looking through my photos, I unexpectedly re-examined the past.

My parents remember my birthday, and old photos help me remember my university days. It wasn’t until the end of the day that I finally realized I should stop and take a look at myself.

A birthday doesn’t lose its meaning just because I missed zero o’clock. It simply reminds me that no matter how busy work is, life shouldn’t consist only of pending tasks. Those who care about me, those who walked this path with me, and myself who is striving to live now, all deserve to be remembered with sincerity.

# Another year older

Next time, I hope we won't have to wait until 10:40 p.m. to realize that this day belongs to us.
