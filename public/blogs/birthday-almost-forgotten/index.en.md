# I almost forgot my birthday.

During my university years, I was very busy and didn’t have many photos or many people I got to know well.

But now, looking back, every person I met was important. The group photos in the album might not have been well taken, and some even have no memory of what happened before or after, but just by looking again, the classrooms, labs, and lively moments during parties seem to come back to me gradually.

![Group photos with friends ](/images/pictures/810c7a01103bde6fd8eccad2b5c5c548131a3acc6d07bed1f7a989322c31d51c.webp)

## A day filled with work

This year’s birthday was rather unusual.

In the morning, when I saw the “Happy Birthday” message from my father, my first thought was: Did they misremember the date?

Then I went to work as usual, focusing on my tasks. The day was completely occupied by work—no cake, no planned outings. By the time I finished work at night and walked home, it was already 11:40. My mother asked me, “Where did you go today for your birthday?”

I was momentarily stunned, and only then did I remember that it really is today.

The saddest thing in the world might be like this: on your birthday, everyone at home remembers, but you forget due to your busyness.

With only twenty minutes left until zero o’clock, I quickly pulled out a few photos from my album to post on Moments, at least to leave something for this day. The photos aren’t chosen yet, and the words aren’t thought out, but the birthday has already passed.

Ah, I’ve aged another year.

## Photos in the album include images from a university not so far in the past

While searching for photos, I accidentally stumbled upon some scenes from my university days

Some people participated in activities together, others worked in the laboratory, and many gathered around a table to eat. In the past, I always thought these scenes were ordinary, that people would just gather and disperse after the event, and the competitions would end after the competition. Now, looking back, I realize that those who were willing to work and take photos together were actually a precious part of that time

Photos left behind some things that I didn’t record carefully at the time. Everyone in the picture is crowded together, smiling at the camera, possibly with unfinished tasks behind them or just finishing a busy period. What exactly happened may become blurred over time, but seeing those familiar faces still reminds me of how I once worked hard with a group of people on certain things.

I didn’t meet many people during my university years, and I’m not particularly good at maintaining lively interpersonal relationships. But the number doesn’t seem to matter much. What truly stays in memory is not how long the contact list is, but who stood beside me during a specific period of time.

## Grew up another year.

This year I didn’t truly celebrate my birthday, and almost missed it. But in the last twenty minutes looking through the album, I unexpectedly reviewed the past again.

My parents remember my birthday, and old photos remind me of my university days. It wasn’t until the end of the day that I finally remembered to stop and look at myself.

Birthdays don’t lose their meaning just because we miss the midnight. They simply remind me that no matter how busy work is, life shouldn’t consist only of pending tasks. Those who care about me, those who have walked this path with me, and the self I strive to live in today, are all worth remembering seriously.

Next time, I hope we won't have to wait until 10:40 p.m. to realize that this day belongs to us.
